Ancestors Awakening — Drum Collection

The 13 lunar moon drums featured on this website are part of the Ancestors Awakening: Neolithic Treasure collection.

The concept, curation, and documentation of the 13-phase lunar moon drum collection are the intellectual property of Christopher Leece. All drum designs and curation concepts are protected under UK copyright law.

The drums were birthed by Edwina Staniforth and the artwork was created by Peter Yankowski. Christopher Leece acts as curator of this collection and is submitting a Guinness World Record application on behalf of the collection for what is believed to be the world's first complete set of 13 lunar moon drums (Guinness application pending adjudication).

All intellectual property rights relating to the drum artwork and construction remain with the respective creators. Any enquiries regarding the collection should be directed to hello@mancsstuff.com.

The Manchester Maze — Interactive Memoir

The Manchester Maze is a free browser-based interactive memoir available at mancsstuff.github.io/The-Manchester-Maze-Game/. It is provided as-is, free of charge, for entertainment and creative-storytelling purposes.

Content Warning & Age Restriction

The game is based on the published memoir of Christopher Ryan Leece and contains references to violence, drug use, bullying, addiction and self-harm. Names, places and events have been fictionalised. Recommended for ages 16 and over. An age confirmation gate appears before play.

Mental health support is signposted within the game (Samaritans 116 123 · text SHOUT to 85258).

The game collects no personal data. There are no cookies, no analytics, no tracking. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages — their standard server logs may apply.
