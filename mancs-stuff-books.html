Memoir · Standalone

The Manchester
Maze

A life lived on the edge of working-class Manchester.

Growing up on a tough Manchester estate in the 1980s — bullying, poverty, gangs. Undiagnosed autism and ADHD. Street life, rave culture, addiction, violence. A debilitating injury. And somehow, through all of it, a way out.

The Manchester Maze is Christopher Leece's own story — raw, honest, and 90% true. A testament to resilience and the stubborn refusal to be beaten by your circumstances.

Working class heritage

The Donkey
Stone

Rag and bone — a tribute to northern working-class life.

The donkey stone was a symbol of northern working-class pride — used to scrub doorsteps clean, a quiet act of dignity in hard times. This book honours that heritage and the people who lived it.

Co-written with Valerie Hickey and illustrated by Christopher Leece — a love letter to the Mancunian spirit.
