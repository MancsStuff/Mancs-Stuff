Furniture &
Product Design

Things built to last, not to impress a client brief.

There's a difference between furniture and a piece of furniture. One fills a room. The other owns it. Every bench, chair, or custom piece we make at Mancs Stuff starts with that distinction in mind.

We restore and upcycle furniture that the world's written off — finding the grain, fixing the bones, and turning it into something you'll want to keep forever. We also take commissions for bespoke pieces designed around your space, your taste, and your budget.

No flatpack. No shortcuts. Just proper craft from someone who knows what it means to work with wood.

What's included

  • Hand restoration of vintage and second-hand pieces
  • Upcycled furniture with a completely new identity
  • Bespoke commissions designed from scratch
  • Hand-finished surfaces — painted, waxed or oiled
  • Delivery available across Greater Manchester
  • UK-wide shipping on smaller pieces

Artwork &
Illustration

Art that looks like it came from somewhere real.

Manchester didn't build its character by playing it safe. Neither does our artwork. Every piece — from small original paintings to limited print runs — carries the same texture, weight and honesty as the city it comes from.

We take commissions for portraits, places and pets. We sell original works and limited print runs. And everything connects to a wider creative world — the #BooksAndBenches project, the lunar moon drum collection, and 7 published books that prove we take storytelling seriously in every medium.

If you want something for your wall that actually means something — not just fills space — you're in the right place.

What we offer

  • Original paintings — A4 to large format
  • Limited edition print runs — signed and numbered
  • Portrait commissions — people, pets, places
  • Artwork inspired by the 13 lunar moon drum project
  • Bespoke pieces for homes, offices and hospitality
  • Secure UK-wide postage on all prints and originals

Logos &
Brand Identity

Your brand should look like you mean it.

Most logos are forgotten within seconds. The ones people remember have a real idea behind them — a mark that reflects the personality of the business, not just what looked nice on a mood board.

We work with small businesses, sole traders and independent creatives who want a brand with genuine character. We don't use templates. We don't recycle ideas. We start with your story — who you are, what you do, who you're for — and build everything outward from there.

We built Mancs Stuff's own brand from scratch. That's your proof of what we can do.

What's included

  • Discovery call to understand your business and vision
  • 2–3 initial logo concepts with rationale
  • Chosen direction refined to final version
  • Files in all formats — SVG, PNG, PDF
  • Brand pack: colours, typography and usage guide
  • Optional: business cards, social media templates

Questions
people
ask us.

Anything else? Email us — we're a real person, not a chatbot.

Do you work with clients outside Manchester?

−

Absolutely — we work UK-wide. Artwork and branding is fully remote. Furniture can be shipped to most UK addresses or collected from Oldham. We're based in Greater Manchester but our work travels.

How long does a project take?

+

A logo typically takes 2–3 weeks. An artwork commission is usually 1–3 weeks. Furniture restoration varies — a basic refresh might be a few days, a bespoke commission 4–6 weeks. We'll always give you a realistic timeline upfront.

Do you require a deposit?

+

Yes — we ask for a 50% deposit before we start, with the remainder due on completion. This protects both of us and lets us get started without delay.

Can I commission something completely custom?

+

That's what we're here for. Furniture built around your exact room, a painting of a specific place, a brand for a business that defies category — bring us your idea and we'll figure it out together.

I have a small budget. Is that OK?

+

Talk to us. We'd rather have an honest conversation and find something that works than turn away a project with real potential. Our pricing starts low because we believe good design shouldn't only be for people with big budgets.
