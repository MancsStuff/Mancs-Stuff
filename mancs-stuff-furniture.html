Bee Bench

Manchester's worker bee — hand-painted, one of a kind.

The Manchester worker bee is the symbol of this city's graft, resilience and community. We've made it our own — hand-restored cast iron frames, freshly cut wood, and the bee painted by hand in the colours of Manchester.

Each MCR bench is completely unique. No two are the same. Available to commission in your colours, your style, or with your name or message on the back.

How it
works.

From your idea to your finished piece in four simple steps.

01

Tell us your idea

Email us with what you want — colours, design, size, any personal touches. Photos of inspiration help but aren't required.

02

We find the frame

We source the right cast iron bench frame — old park benches, salvage yard finds. The older the better. Then we strip it back to the bones.

03

We build and paint

New wood slats, fresh paint on the ironwork, and your design hand-painted on by us. We'll send you progress photos along the way.

04

Delivered to you

We deliver across Greater Manchester or arrange shipping UK-wide. Every bench comes ready to go straight outside.
