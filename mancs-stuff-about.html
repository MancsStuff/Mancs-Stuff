Our story
Who we are

Not your average
design studio.

Mancs Stuff Ltd was born in Oldham from something simple: a belief that design should have soul, grit, and a story behind it. Not polished-for-the-sake-of-it. Not corporate. Proper creative work made by human hands in the north of England.

We're a specialist design company covering three things we genuinely love: furniture and product design, original artwork and illustration, and logos and brand identity. Every piece we make — whether it's a restored bench, a painting, or a brand mark — carries the same DNA: authentic, bold, and unmistakably Mancunian.

We're also, it should be said, the team behind what may become the world's first collection of 13 lunar moon drums — a Guinness World Record attempt that tells you everything you need to know about how we approach our work. If we're going to do something, we do it properly. And we don't stop until it's extraordinary.

What
we
do.

Three disciplines. One creative vision. All made with care in Greater Manchester.

01

Furniture & product design

We restore, upcycle and reimagine furniture into statement pieces. Each one is hand-finished, one of a kind, and built to last. From benches to bespoke commissions — if it's made of wood and needs a second life, we're your people.

02

Artwork & illustration

Original paintings, limited prints, and commissions with real character. Our work is influenced by the textures of Manchester — industrial, warm, and a little bit unexpected. Hang something on your wall that actually means something.

03

Logos & brand identity

We help small businesses and sole traders find their visual voice. No corporate templates, no generic clip art. Just honest, distinctive brand work designed around who you actually are — because the best brands have a story worth telling.

Currently in progress

Guinness
World
Record
Attempt.

We're currently working towards what we believe will be the world's first complete set of 13 lunar moon drums — one for each phase of the moon.

It's an art project, a craft challenge, and a record attempt all in one. But more than anything, it's proof that we approach every single thing we make with the same question: what would happen if we went all the way?

Stay tuned. This one's going to be something special.

The person behind it

Chris is a Mancunian maker, designer, and published author of 7 books. He's spent years working with his hands — restoring furniture, building things, fixing things — and somewhere along the way realised that the same instincts that make a good handyman make a good designer: attention to detail, respect for materials, and caring about the finished result.

Mancs Stuff Ltd is the home for all of that creativity. It's where the furniture restoration meets the brand design meets the artwork meets the record attempt. It's not a conventional design studio because Chris isn't a conventional designer — and that's exactly the point.

He also runs Chris Can Handyman, a Oldham-based sole trader service, and the #BooksAndBenches project — proof that the best creative businesses grow from doing real work in the real world.
