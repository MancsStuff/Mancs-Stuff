Mancs Stuff was built from the ground up in Oldham by a craftsman, author, and artist who believes design should have soul — not just style. We make furniture that lasts, art that means something, and brands that look like the businesses behind them.

We're the same people who restored the bench in your local café, wrote 7 books, and are currently attempting a Guinness World Record with 13 hand-built lunar moon drums. Creativity isn't a job title here — it's just how we live.

World's
First
13 Lunar
Moon
Drums.

13 hand-crafted lunar moon drums — one for each phase of the moon. Birthed by Edwina Staniforth, artwork by Peter Yankowski — Ancestors Awakening: Neolithic Treasure collection.

Christopher Leece curated this extraordinary collection and is submitting a Guinness World Record application for the what is believed to be the world's first complete set of 13 lunar moon drums (Guinness application pending adjudication) — helping bring this remarkable work to a global audience.

Status: Guinness application in progress · Pending adjudication · 2026
