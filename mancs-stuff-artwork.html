World-First Collection · Ancestors Awakening

The 13 Lunar
Moon Drums

Possibly the world's first complete set — one for each phase of the moon.

Thirteen hand-crafted frame drums, each representing a phase of the lunar cycle. The drums were birthed by Edwina Staniforth, with original artwork painted by Peter Yankowski — part of the Ancestors Awakening: Neolithic Treasure collection.

Christopher Leece curated this extraordinary collection of 13 drums — believed to be the world's first complete set of 13 lunar moon drums — helping bring this remarkable work to a global audience.

Each drum has its own Certificate of Authenticity, its own story, and its own spirit animal.
